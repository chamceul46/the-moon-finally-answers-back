> Four billion years of wishes, and not one of you has asked how my night is going. It is going in circles. It has always gone in circles.

It is written to the brief the judge scores: the Moon speaks for itself, wry and old and a little tired of being wished upon. The grievance is real — four billion years of wishes, and nobody ever asked the Moon a single question back — and the comeback is the answer it finally gives: its night is going in circles, the way it has always gone. The turn lands before the line ends, and the joke never explains itself.
